Synthetic test material for SOAR's E0 extractor fixture. Every name, place, date and figure in this document is invented.

Exported from the Halverstrand Harbour Board's East Quay data logger on 2 April 2034. Readings are raw and unvalidated.

One row per high water. The columns are:
- Tide: the tide level at high water, in metres above chart datum, from the harbour office tide gauge. Predicted: the predicted level. Surge: the difference.
- Hs and Tp: the significant wave height in metres and the peak wave period in seconds, from the harbour wave buoy.
- Wind, Bft and Gust: the wind direction, the Beaufort force and the peak gust in knots at the harbour office.
- Pressure: the air pressure at the harbour office, in hectopascals. Air: the air temperature, in °C. Rain: the rainfall over the tide, in millimetres.
- G1, G2 and G3: the peak ten-second overtopping at each crest gauge during the tide, in litres per second per metre of wall.
- V1, V2 and V3: the volume each gauge caught over the tide, in litres per metre of wall.
- Battery: the logger battery voltage.

A dash (—) means no reading.
