# Halverstrand Harbour Board: public notice on the East Quay seawall trial

Synthetic test material for SOAR's E0 extractor fixture. Every name, place, date and figure in this document is invented.

Notice HHB-2034-07, issued on 16 January 2034.

The Board has completed the first stage of its seawall trial on the East Quay. Between 3 March and 29 August 2033 the contractor built 140 metres of stepped concrete seawall with a recurved crest. It replaces the vertical timber wall that had stood on the quay since 1958.

The trial asks whether a recurved crest can keep winter spray and green water off the quay road without raising the wall. The new crest sits at the same height as the old wall, 4.1 metres above chart datum.

## What the gauges show

Before the works, the Board’s gauges logged mean overtopping of 4.2 litres per second per metre of wall during the five largest storms of the 2032–33 winter.

Over the five largest storms of the 2033–34 winter so far, the mean was 0.9 litres per second per metre.

The quay road was closed for 11 days in the winter before the works, and for 2 days so far this winter.

These figures are provisional. They come from two of the three gauges on the new wall, and the winter is not over. The full report of the Board's monitoring consultant is expected in April 2034.

## Cost

The trial cost 2.6 million crowns. The regional coastal fund paid 70 per cent, and the Board paid the rest from its harbour dues.

## Next steps

The Board will decide in May 2034 whether to extend the wall along the remaining 380 metres of the East Quay.

Residents may comment at a public meeting in the harbour office on 12 February 2034. The Board will publish a summary of the comments with its decision.
