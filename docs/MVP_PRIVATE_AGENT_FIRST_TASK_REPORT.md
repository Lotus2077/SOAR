decision and report, then asserted that the decision equaled the same constant.
That test confirmed consistency with its own draft rather than independently
deriving the answer from the source terms. A second hypothetical calculation
repeated the error. The report also overstated the effect of small fees despite
remaining budget headroom.

The host's configured source-preservation/file-readability checks passed. Those
checks do not establish numerical truth, and the session correctly remained
`submitted` with independent acceptance pending. The independent critical checker
failed at `annual_costs`; later checker assertions were not executed and must not
be reported as passed. A separate reviewer reached the arithmetic failure without
reading evaluator gold. The final acceptance join records `artifactAccepted: false`.
